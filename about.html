Profile

Adv. Sadiq Adan Ali

Adv. Sadiq Adan Ali is a dedicated legal professional and data practitioner with extensive experience in litigation, legal advisory, corporate and commercial law, conflict resolution, and social justice.

With over 1,450 cases handled and a strong success record, he combines legal expertise with data analysis and research capabilities to deliver evidence-based solutions for individuals, businesses, organizations, and communities.

He is committed to promoting access to justice, child protection, gender-based violence prevention, women's empowerment, and sustainable peacebuilding throughout Somalia.

Licensed to practice before the Supreme Courts of the Federal Republic of Somalia (2024), Galmudug State (2021), and Puntland (2020), Adv. Sadiq brings a nationally recognised legal standing across multiple jurisdictions.

Beyond the courtroom, he leads Somali Lawyers Without Borders as CEO and serves as General Director of the Galmudug Bar Association — roles through which he advances legal aid, capacity building, and institutional development.
